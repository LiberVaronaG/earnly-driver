# Earnly Driver — Support

**Need help?** This page covers the most common questions. If your question isn't answered below, email **megashoponline826@gmail.com** and we'll get back to you within 2 business days.

---

## Quick start

1. **Open Earnly Driver** and grant location access when prompted. Choose **Allow While Using App** first; when you start your first shift, iOS will then ask you to upgrade to **Allow Always** so the app can track you in the background.
2. **Tap Start Shift** when your Amazon Flex block begins. The app starts a timer, tracks your route, and shows a Live Activity on your Lock Screen.
3. **Drive your block** as normal. You don't need to keep the app open — it works in the background.
4. **Tap End Shift** when you finish. The app calculates your earnings per hour, cost per mile, and net profit.
5. **Review your stats** in the Earnings tab. Export to CSV any time from Settings.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Earnly Driver work with Amazon Flex automatically?
No. Earnly Driver is an independent app and does not connect to Amazon Flex. You start and end shifts manually by tapping the buttons in the app. Amazon Flex does not provide a public API; no third-party app can track Flex shifts automatically.

### Does the app share my data with Amazon?
No. Earnly Driver does not communicate with Amazon, Amazon Flex, or any other service. All your data stays on your iPhone. See our [Privacy Policy](/privacy/) for full details.

### Why does it ask for "Always" location access?
So the app can keep tracking your route after you put your phone in your pocket, lock the screen, or switch to the Amazon Flex app while delivering. Location is only used **while a shift is active** — never before you tap Start Shift, never after you tap End Shift. You can verify this in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Earnly Driver**.

### Will it drain my battery?
We've optimized location tracking to use the most efficient iOS APIs available. During an active 4-hour shift you should expect roughly 4–6% battery drain from Earnly Driver on a modern iPhone. If you see significantly more, please email us with your iPhone model and we'll investigate.

### Can I edit a shift after I end it?
Yes. Open any shift in the History tab and tap Edit. You can adjust earnings, tips, mileage, and notes. The shift's recorded start/end times and GPS route are preserved for accuracy.

### What happens if I forget to tap Start Shift?
You can add a shift manually from the History tab. Tap the **+** button and enter the date, times, and earnings by hand. GPS-tracked metrics won't be available for manually added shifts, but everything else (earnings/hour, taxes, etc.) calculates normally.

### What happens if I forget to tap End Shift?
The app will keep tracking until you remember. When you reopen it, just tap End Shift — you can then edit the end time to the correct value. If you let it run all night, your shift will show inflated duration; edit the end time on the shift's detail screen to fix it.

### Can I export my data for taxes?
Yes. Go to **Settings → Export Data**. The app generates a CSV with every shift, its earnings, mileage, hours, and date. You can share it via email, save it to Files, or AirDrop it to your Mac. The CSV format is compatible with Excel, Numbers, Google Sheets, and most tax prep tools.

### Does the app track expenses?
Yes. You can log fuel, maintenance, tolls, and other vehicle expenses from the Expenses tab. These are subtracted from your gross earnings to calculate net profit. Mileage from GPS-tracked shifts is automatically available for the standard IRS mileage deduction.

### Does the app handle taxes?
Earnly Driver helps you **track** earnings, mileage, and expenses in the format needed for tax preparation. It does **not** file taxes for you, calculate quarterly estimated tax payments, or provide tax advice. Consult a tax professional for your specific situation.

### Will my data sync between iPhones?
Not in the current version. Your data is stored locally on the iPhone where you record it. If you switch iPhones, restore from an iCloud or encrypted local backup of your old phone — the app's data is included in standard iPhone backups. We're evaluating optional iCloud sync for a future version.

### What iPhones does it support?
iPhone running **iOS 18.5 or later**. This means iPhone XS / XR (2018) and newer. iPhone X and older are not supported because they cannot install iOS 18.

### Is there an Android version?
Not currently. Earnly Driver is iOS-only. An Android version is not on our near-term roadmap.

### Does it support DoorDash, Uber, Instacart, etc.?
The current version is focused on **Amazon Flex** specifically. We made this choice intentionally — building one app that does Flex really well, before trying to handle every platform poorly. Support for other gig platforms may come in a future major version.

### Can I see my route on a map?
Yes. Open any completed shift in the History tab and you'll see the full route drawn on a map, with start point, end point, and the path taken.

### I tapped End Shift but my earnings show $0
Earnings are not auto-detected (Amazon Flex doesn't expose them via API). You enter the dollar amount yourself when you end the shift, or by editing the shift afterward. Open the shift in History → Edit → Earnings.

### The app crashed / shows wrong data / froze
Email **megashoponline826@gmail.com** with:
- iPhone model and iOS version (Settings → General → About)
- What you were doing when it happened
- Approximate date and time of the issue

If your data appears wrong but the app is otherwise working, try force-closing and reopening (swipe up from the bottom of the screen and flick Earnly Driver away). If the issue persists, email us.

### How do I delete all my data?
Delete the app from your iPhone (long-press the icon → Remove App → Delete App). All data is removed with the app. There are no servers to clear; nothing is retained anywhere.
